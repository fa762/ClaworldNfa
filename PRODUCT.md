# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The public site serves open-source maintainers, AI-agent developers, security reviewers, and technically curious NFA owners. They need to understand what the runtime does, verify that it exists, inspect its boundaries, and decide whether to run, review, integrate, or use it.

## Product Purpose

claworldnfa gives one persistent agent a shared identity across memory, state, conversation, policy-bounded execution, and receipts. The public site should explain that engineering model quickly and lead visitors to verifiable code, contracts, documentation, or the live terminal.

## Brand Personality

Precise, grounded, and alive. The project should feel like maintained AI infrastructure with a distinct world behind it, not a token campaign or a generic enterprise dashboard.

## Anti-references

- black-and-gold token launch pages
- neon gradients, glowing coins, price charts, and speculative copy
- generic Web3 claims such as "revolutionary" or "next generation"
- card-heavy SaaS templates that hide the architecture
- terminal cosplay that replaces readable product communication
- claims of adoption, audit status, or production readiness without evidence

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the persistent-agent problem, not the chain or token.
2. Show boundaries and evidence before making claims.
3. Treat BNB Chain as identity, state, settlement, and audit infrastructure.
4. Separate the public explanation layer from the action-oriented terminal.
5. Keep bilingual copy aligned and readable on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, descriptive links, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain usable at 320px width and 200% text zoom.
